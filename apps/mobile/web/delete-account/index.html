How to ask

  1. In the app or at tajirika.co.ke: sign in, then Account → Close account → Ask to close my account.
  2. Without signing in: write to [TO CONFIRM: support email] from the email address on your account, or call [TO CONFIRM: support number] from your registered phone number. We will check it is you before acting.

What happens

  • Any balance is paid to your M-Pesa number first. Money still moving (a top-up or payout in progress) must finish.
  • We then close the account, usually within [TO CONFIRM: number] working days. You are signed out everywhere and cannot sign in, play, top up or withdraw.
  • Your password and sign-in links stop working, and every session on every device ends.

What we must keep

The law requires gambling operators to keep some records after an account closes: payments, plays, prizes, tax withheld, identity checks and anti-money-laundering records. We keep these, secured and seen only by people who need them, for the periods the law sets [TO CONFIRM: the periods]. Then we delete or anonymise them. See our privacy policy.

Only want to stop playing?

A break or self-exclusion stops play at once, without closing your account. See Responsible gambling.
