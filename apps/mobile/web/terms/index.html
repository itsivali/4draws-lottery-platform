2. Definitions

  • Account: your 4draws account, identified by your M-Pesa phone number.
  • Wallet: the balance of money in your account that the Service has confirmed.
  • Top-up: a payment from your M-Pesa into your wallet.
  • Play: one paid spin of the wheel.
  • Draw chance: one entry in a draw, earned by a play.
  • Draw: a daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly selection of winners from the draw chances entered in it.
  • Prize: money awarded to a winner of a draw.
  • The Regulator: the authority that licenses and supervises gambling in Kenya.

3. Who may open an account

  • You must be 18 years or older. Gambling by anyone under 18 is against the law.
  • You must be in Kenya when you play.
  • You may hold one account only, opened in your own name.
  • Your M-Pesa number must be registered in your own name.
  • You may not use the Service while you are self-excluded or on a break, or if a court or the Regulator has barred you from gambling.
  • Our directors, staff and their immediate families, and anyone involved in running the draws, may not play.

4. Your account and its security

  • Give true, complete information and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your password secret and your devices secure. Do not let anyone else use your account.
  • You are responsible for what is done through your account until you tell us it has been compromised. Tell us at once if you think it has.
  • We will never ask for your M-Pesa PIN. Do not give it to anyone who says they are from 4draws.
  • We may limit the number of devices signed in to an account, and you can see and sign out your devices in the app.

5. Verifying who you are

The law requires us to know our customers. We may ask for your names, national ID number and date of birth, and for proof of them, at any time and always before your first withdrawal or prize payout. Your first M-Pesa top-up confirms that your phone number is yours. If we cannot verify you, or what you gave us turns out to be untrue, we may restrict or close the account and deal with any balance as the law requires.

6. Your wallet and top-ups

  • Top-ups are made from M-Pesa only, from the number on your account, by approving the M-Pesa prompt or paying to our paybill.
  • The smallest and largest top-up, and any deposit limits you have set, are shown in the app.
  • Your wallet shows only money the Service has confirmed with M-Pesa. A top-up that M-Pesa later reverses is taken back from your wallet; if you have already spent it, it is owed and your next top-up repays it first.
  • Your wallet is not a bank account. It earns no interest, we give no credit, and it is for playing only.
  • Money in your wallet remains yours and can be withdrawn under clause 9.

7. Plays

  • Each play costs the price shown before you confirm it, and is paid from your wallet or, if you choose, directly from your M-Pesa.
  • The number of plays allowed each day is shown in the app.
  • The result of a play is decided by our servers using a method you can check afterwards (the fairness page in the app explains how). The wheel on your screen only shows that result.
  • A play earns draw chances for that day. A play that lands on a lucky slot doubles that day's draw chances.
  • The wheel never pays cash. Prizes come only from the draws.
  • A confirmed play cannot be cancelled or refunded, except under clause 13.
  • Most plays do not win a prize. Play for entertainment and only with money you can afford to lose.

8. Draws and prizes

  • Your draw chances are entered in that day's draw and in your own weekly, monthly and quarterly draws. Your periods start on the day of your first play and are shown in the app.
  • Winners are selected using a public source of randomness that anyone can check, and each draw is approved by two officials before its results stand. Results are published in the app.
  • Prizes and how they are shared are shown in the app for each draw before it is run.
  • A prize is paid to the M-Pesa number on the winner's account, after identity verification and less any tax the law requires us to withhold.
  • If we cannot pay a prize because the winner cannot be verified or reached, it is held and dealt with as the law requires.

9. Withdrawals and payouts

  • You can ask to withdraw your wallet balance at any time, to the M-Pesa number on your account only.
  • Your identity must be verified before your first withdrawal.
  • Withdrawal limits are shown in the app. Large or unusual payouts are reviewed by our staff before they are sent, which can delay them.
  • We may hold a payment while we look into it, where the anti-money-laundering law or the Regulator requires.
  • M-Pesa's own limits and charges apply to what you receive.

10. Fees and taxes
