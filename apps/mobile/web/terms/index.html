Who may play

  • You must be 18 or older and in Kenya.
  • One account per person. Your M-Pesa number must be registered in your own name.
  • You may not play if you have excluded yourself from gambling.

Your account

Keep your password and devices secure. We may ask you to prove who you are at any time, and always before paying you. We may suspend an account to protect players or to meet our legal duties.

Playing

Each play costs the price shown before you confirm it. Results are produced as described on the in-app "How it works" and fairness pages, where you can check them. Prizes are paid to your M-Pesa number, less any tax the law requires us to withhold.

Money

Top-ups and withdrawals go through M-Pesa only. Withdrawals have limits and may be checked before they are sent. We may hold money while we look into a payment, as the anti-money-laundering law requires.

Responsible gambling

Set deposit limits, take a break or exclude yourself at any time in the app. See Responsible gambling.
