How it works

  1. Create your account with the M-Pesa number you will play with. The M-Pesa prompt of your first top-up confirms the number is yours.
  2. Top up from KES 1. A prompt arrives on your phone; approve it and the money is in your wallet.
  3. Spin the wheel, up to four times a day, at KES 1 a spin. Every spin enters you in the draws.
  4. Prizes are credited to your wallet and withdrawn to your M-Pesa; the first withdrawal needs your national ID verified.

Fair draws, on the record

Each spin is committed to before it happens and revealed afterwards, and draws use a public randomness beacon. You can check every result yourself from the Fairness page in the app, and the protocol and verifier are published for anyone to run. Draws are signed and approved by two officials before prizes are paid.

Play responsibly

4draws is for adults of 18 and over. Lottery plays cost money and most plays do not win a prize. You can set deposit limits, take a break or self-exclude at any time from your account.

Questions

How much does a spin cost?
KES 1 a spin, up to four spins a day, paid from your wallet, which you top up from M-Pesa from KES 1.
How do I get paid?
Prizes go to your wallet and are withdrawn to the M-Pesa number you play with, once your national ID is verified.
Which draws can I enter?
The daily draw, and weekly, monthly and quarterly draws over your own playing periods. Every spin counts.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes: it runs in any modern browser, and as an app on Android and iOS.
