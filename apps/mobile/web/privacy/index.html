How we keep it safe

Identity details are encrypted. Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes. Staff see only what their role needs, and viewing identity documents is recorded.

Who we share it with

  • Safaricom (M-Pesa), to make and check payments.
  • Our hosting and network providers, who run the service for us. Today the service is hosted in the United States; it is moving to hosting in Kenya, as the gambling regulations require. [TO CONFIRM: the transfer safeguards]
  • Google or Apple, if you choose to sign in with them.
  • The authorities the law requires us to report to: the gambling regulator, the Financial Reporting Centre and the Kenya Revenue Authority.

How long we keep it

While your account is open, and afterwards for the periods the law sets for payment, gambling and tax records [TO CONFIRM: the periods]. After that it is deleted or made anonymous.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2019 you can ask to see your data, to have it corrected, to have it deleted where the law allows, and to object to how it is used. Write to [TO CONFIRM: privacy contact email]. To close your account, see Delete your account. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Children

Tajirika is only for people aged 18 and over. We refuse sign-ups from anyone who says they are younger, and we check identity before paying anyone.
