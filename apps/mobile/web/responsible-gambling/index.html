Responsible gambling

Draft. This page is a draft prepared for review by the operator's lawyers and is not yet final. Tajirika is not yet licensed by Kenya's gambling regulator and does not operate real-money gambling: the service currently runs as a demonstration with M-Pesa's test environment. Items marked [TO CONFIRM] are open.

Gambling should be entertainment, never a way to make money or to recover losses. Only play with money you can afford to lose.

Tools in the app

  • Deposit limits, daily, weekly and monthly. Lowering one takes effect at once; raising one waits 24 hours.
  • A break of 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. Plays and top-ups stop, and the break cannot be ended early.
  • Self-exclusion for 6 months, 1 year or permanently. You are signed out everywhere, plays and top-ups stop, and nobody can shorten or lift it. You can still withdraw your balance. You can also ask our support staff to exclude you.

Find them under Account → Limits, breaks and self-exclusion.

Signs to watch for

  • Spending more than you planned, or chasing losses.
  • Borrowing, or using money meant for food, rent or school fees.
  • Hiding your gambling from family, or feeling anxious when you are not playing.
